Delete your MDRNJ Fitness account

MDRNJ Fitness · student senior project, California State University, Long Beach

You can delete your MDRNJ Fitness account and its data at any time, with or without the app.

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

  1. Open MDRNJ Fitness and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account, confirm, and type DELETE.

Your account is deleted right away and you're signed out.

Option 2: by email (no app needed)

Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my MDRNJ Fitness account". Include your username if you know it. We'll confirm and delete the account within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email, username, display name, password hash, Google sign-in link) and sign-in sessions
  • Your profile (bio, weight, height, fitness goal), hero and gear
  • Your workouts, XP, levels, stats, gold, quests, achievements and duel history
  • Your friends, friend requests and blocks
  • Your chat messages, the reports and feedback you sent, and your challenges with their proof videos and photos

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Copies in encrypted database backups for up to 35 days, after which they're overwritten.
  • Standard server logs (such as IP address and request time) for a limited time for security and troubleshooting.

Want only some of your data deleted, or a copy of it first? Email us at the address above. See the full Privacy Policy.
