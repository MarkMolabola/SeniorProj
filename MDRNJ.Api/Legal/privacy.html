MDRNJ Fitness ("the app", "we", "us") is a gamified fitness app built as a student senior project at California State University, Long Beach. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. If you have questions, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

The short version: we collect what the app needs to run your account, your workouts and the social features. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking. You can delete your account and data from inside the app at any time.

1. Information we collect

WhatWhy

The app asks for camera and microphone access only when you record a challenge proof, and photo library access only when you pick one. It does not access your location, contacts or health records.

2. Who can see your information

  • Other players can see your username, display name, hero, level, stats, recent workouts and leaderboard position, and anything you post in chats you share with them.
  • Challenge proofs are visible only to the two players in that challenge, through short-lived private links.
  • Moderators (app admins) can see reported messages and the account that posted them, and can remove messages, mute or ban accounts.
  • Service providers that run the app for us, and only for that purpose:
    • Microsoft Azure (hosting, database and file storage, in the United States)
    • Azure Communication Services (sending verification and password-reset emails)
    • Google (only if you choose Google sign-in)
  • We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our users.

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

3. Information stored on your device

The app keeps your sign-in tokens in your device's secure storage and caches some of your data (such as your profile and recent activity) so it loads quickly and works offline. Signing out or deleting the app removes it.

4. How long we keep it

  • Challenge proofs are deleted automatically once they've served their purpose: as soon as a reviewed challenge is settled or disputed, otherwise 24 hours after the challenge ends. Any upload that was never used is deleted within 10 days.
  • Everything else is kept while your account exists.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your account, profile, workouts, game progress, gear, friendships, blocks, chat messages, reports you made, and your challenges with their proofs. Copies may remain in encrypted backups for a limited time (typically up to 35 days) before they're overwritten.
  • Server logs are kept for a limited time for security and troubleshooting.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile at any time in the app.
  • Delete your account in the app: Profile → Delete account. This takes effect immediately.
  • Block or report other players from any chat message or profile.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. California residents have these rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, and we honor them for all users. We won't treat you differently for using them.

6. Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords and sign-in tokens are stored only as secure hashes, and proof files are kept in private storage that can't be browsed publicly. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

7. Children

MDRNJ Fitness is not intended for children under 13, and you must be 13 or older to create an account. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes, we'll update the version and date above and ask you to review and accept the new policy in the app.
